MakanLah recommends restaurants in Kuala Lumpur. Every pick shows the post it came from.

It doesn't start with a search box. It asks what you're craving, who you're with, and how far you're willing to go.

Then it reads what people actually wrote. Real posts, from RedNote and Google Maps, in English, Malay and Chinese, often in the same sentence.

Every result carries the words someone wrote. Not a generated summary. The post itself, quoted, with a link back to it.

That's the difference. A recommendation you can check.
